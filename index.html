Other projects

  • Sustainable Spaces shelter

    Academic, ATU Sligo, 2026 · SolidWorks

    A concept design for a sustainable outdoor shelter: a green-roofed structure with four hexagon-shaped entrances, seating inside and a central pond with glass underneath so people can look into it. The roof catches rainwater over 8.22 m² and feeds tanks holding 10,480 litres, with a drainage system for overflow. I produced the full drawing, including section views and a materials schedule: granite flooring, a low-carbon steel frame and foam seating.

  • Micro:bit RC car

    Personal, 2026, ongoing · SolidWorks, 3D printing, micro:bit, Python

    A small remote-controlled car I designed in SolidWorks and 3D printed, including the chassis, wheels, axle and servo-driven steering linkage. A micro:bit drives the motor and steering servo, and a Python script on a laptop reads the arrow keys and sends drive and steering commands over a serial link.

  • 2D multibody dynamics simulator

    Personal, 2026 · MATLAB

    A MATLAB script that assembles rigid bodies and joints into equations of motion, integrates them with ode113 and renders the result as an animation. Joint constraints are held with Baumgarte stabilisation. I tested it on pendulums and linked chains of up to four bodies, where total energy drifted by less than one part in a billion over eight simulated seconds, and extended it to two-body gravity with collision detection.

  • Micro:bit step counter with Python analysis

    Leaving Certificate Computer Science, 2024 · micro:bit, Python

    A micro:bit step counter that logs daily steps, with a Python program that validates the exported CSV data, fits a linear trend to predict future step counts, and scores sleep and mood inputs to give wellbeing feedback with bar and pie charts.

About

I’m a mechanical engineering student based in Ireland, focused on mechanical design, simulation and system integration. I take designs from concept through CAD, analysis and validation, using SolidWorks, CATIA, ANSYS, Abaqus and MATLAB/Simulink. I also 3D print a lot, with FDM and resin printers at home and daily in industry.

My projects cover autonomous systems, drivetrain and gear mechanisms, and structural chassis design, with performance checked through material, stress and motion analysis. I’m looking for engineering roles in mechanical design, numerical simulation and analytical validation, especially where mechanical, electrical and control systems meet.

Outside engineering I ride motorbikes and motocross, competed in the Ferrari Esports Challenge in 2023, and spend time on CAD modelling, 3D game design and personal engineering projects.
