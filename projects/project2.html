Autonomous taxi robot

A line-following robot taxi that navigates a taped course, detects junctions and drives to a selected destination. I led the team and wrote the control software.

Type
Academic, group
Year
2024
My role
Team leader, lead programmer
Tools
Arduino Uno, C++, SolidWorks
Sheet
Project 2 of 4

Objective

Design and build an autonomous line-following taxi prototype that can drive to one of several destinations.

Method

A four-wheel-drive platform is controlled by an Arduino Uno. Line sensors track the black-tape path, junction detection and destination selection are written in C++, and LiDAR input is used to detect and avoid obstacles. The project ran on weekly milestones.

Results

The robot tracked the line across the full course, detected junctions reliably and reached the destination selected with its hardware inputs.

Conclusion

The robot navigated the course repeatably and the control logic worked as intended under test conditions.
