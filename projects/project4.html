Differential gearbox motion study

A differential gearbox modelled and assembled in SolidWorks, with a motion study of how it splits speed between two output shafts.

Objective

Design a differential gearbox and analyse its kinematic behaviour.

Method

Each component was modelled and assembled in SolidWorks with correct mates and clearances, then a motion study was used to analyse the relative speeds of the two output shafts.

Results

The differential behaved correctly and no parts interfered during motion.

Conclusion

The gearbox works as expected and the model is ready for further analysis or modification.
